## Design Context

### Users
- **Target Audience**: Final Fantasy XIV players who want to showcase their screenshots and character specs.
- **Context**: Social media sharing (X/Twitter, Discord) and community galleries.
- **Emotional Goals**: Pride in their character's appearance ("My character looks amazing"), trust in professional-looking info ("This looks like a premium card"), and a sense of simplicity and polish.

### Brand Personality
- **Voice**: Minimal, Intuitive, Premium.
- **Tone**: Warm Minimalism. It should feel like a high-end print publication, not a clinical tech tool.
- **3-Word Personality**: Sophisticated, Craft-centered, Effortless.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Primary Inspiration**: Cursor.com (Warm off-white, warm near-black text, refined typography).
- **Core Visuals**: Warm cream surfaces (`#f2f1ed`), oklab-based warm brown borders, and subtle atmospheric shadows.
- **Theme**: Supports both Light and Dark modes, but the "Signature" look is the Warm Light mode (Cursor Cream).
- **Key Constraints**: 
  - **UI Typography**: Strictly use `Pretendard` for all UI elements (Sidebar, Buttons, Modals) to maintain a professional and consistent interface.
  - **Card Typography**: Users can select custom fonts (A2z, GMarket, etc.) **only** for the text rendered inside the character card preview.
  - **Premium Polish**: No "AI-slop" patterns (no side-stripe borders, no neon gradients, no generic rounded rectangles).

### Design Principles
1. **Typographic Hierarchy over Weight**: Use font size and negative tracking (letter-spacing) for hierarchy rather than heavy bold weights.
2. **Intentional Spacing**: Use an 8px grid with micro-increments (1.5px, 2px) for perfect alignment of icons and text.
3. **Warmth & Texture**: Avoid pure white/black. Every surface should feel "warm" to evoke a sense of craft and premium quality.
4. **Context-Aware Sidebar**: The sidebar should be a structured, editorial-style controls panel, not a messy list of inputs. Use grouping and clear typography.
5. **Rock-Solid Rendering**: The card preview must be pixel-perfect and consistent across all environments during image export.
